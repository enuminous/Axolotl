# Limitations and Falsification

AXOLOTL is an experimental adaptation framework.

## Limitations

- Function scores are domain-dependent.
- Transformation distance depends on representation.
- A novel state may contain hidden liabilities.
- Adaptation cost can be difficult to normalize.
- Improvement after damage does not prove the damage caused beneficial learning.
- Synthetic adaptation is not evidence of biological regeneration.

## Falsification criteria

AXOLOTL's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Conventional recovery metrics predict outcomes equally well.
2. Transformation distance adds no useful information.
3. Learning efficiency fails to predict future adaptation.
4. Adaptations fail on held-out disturbances.
5. Invariant retention does not distinguish safe from unsafe adaptation.
6. Results depend strongly on arbitrary state encodings.

## Claim boundary

AXOLOTL evaluates adaptive recovery in specified models. It does not imply biological regeneration, autonomous self-improvement, or universal resilience.
